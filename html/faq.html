Reservations & Seating

4

Reservations can be made online through our Reservation page, by phone at +998-8776345, or by emailing zest.co@gmail.com. We recommend booking at least 48 hours in advance for weekend and holiday dining to guarantee your preferred table.

Our main dining hall comfortably seats parties from 2 to 12 guests. For larger groups of 13 or more, please contact our events team directly so we can arrange the perfect private or semi-private space for your celebration.

We hold reservations for up to 15 minutes past the booked time. If you are running late, please call ahead and we will do our best to accommodate. For no-shows on special-occasion bookings, a courtesy rebooking is required.

No deposit is required for standard tables. However, for the Chef's Private Tasting Counter, the outdoor terrace, and all private dining experiences, a per-guest deposit is applied and credited toward your final bill.

Delivery & Takeout

3

We currently deliver within a 12km radius of our Karachi location. You can also place a takeout order for pickup at the restaurant — simply choose "Pickup" during checkout and we'll have it ready when you arrive.

Most deliveries arrive within 40–50 minutes. During peak hours, this may extend slightly. You'll receive live order updates via SMS and email so you always know exactly when your feast lands at your door.

We accept all major credit and debit cards, mobile wallets, and cash on delivery. In-restaurant we also support Apple Pay and Google Pay for a seamless experience.
